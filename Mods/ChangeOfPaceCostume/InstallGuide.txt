========================================
  Chill with You: Lo-Fi Story
  Change of Pace - Costume Mod v1.7.1
========================================

【功能简介】

本 Mod 为游戏的"转换心情"（Change of Pace）装饰面板新增三类功能：

一、服装切换：可以直接更换角色服装，并可选择"一次 / 一日 / 一生"三种请求模式。
二、发型切换：通常发型与发夹 A/B/C。发型设置后遵循游戏默认规则，一天内保持、每日轮换。（“贝雷帽”特殊发型，装有贝雷帽时无法修改发型。）
三、限定装饰常驻：魔法公主贝雷帽、圣诞帽、愚人节墨镜可以随时装备且自动保持。戴上头部装饰（贝雷帽 / 圣诞帽）时会自动临时替换猫耳耳机；贝雷帽与圣诞帽属于同一部位、互相排斥，同时只能佩戴一顶。

This mod adds three features to the "Change of Pace" decoration panel:

1. Costume switching: change outfits directly, with three request modes (Once / One day / Forever).
2. Hairstyle switching: Normal and hairpins A/B/C. After you set a style, it follows the game's default rules — kept for the day, rotated daily. (The beret is a special hair state: hair cannot be changed while the beret is worn.)
3. Permanent limited decorations: Magical Princess beret, Christmas hat, and April Fools sunglasses can be equipped at any time and are kept automatically. Headwear (beret / Christmas hat) temporarily replaces cat-ear headphones; the beret and the Christmas hat share the same slot and are mutually exclusive.

2. Extract the whole archive into the game folder (same level as Chill With You.exe).
3. Launch the game.

[Install B: BepInEx already installed, or upgrading from an older version]

1. Download the mod-only package ChangeOfPaceCostume.zip (does not include BepInEx).
2. Extract it and copy ChangeOfPaceCostume.dll into BepInEx\plugins\ in the game folder, overwriting the old file.
3. Launch the game; your previous settings (costumes / decoration toggles) are kept automatically.

【日志与故障诊断】

如果安装后游戏内没有出现 Mod 功能，请按以下步骤获取日志：

1. 日志文件位置：游戏目录\BepInEx\LogOutput.log。
2. 请先正常关闭游戏（不要直接结束进程），再用记事本打开该文件；也可以直接把整个文件发送给作者。
3. 在日志中搜索 "Change of Pace - Costume"，将包含该关键词的整段日志复制给作者即可。

正常加载时，日志中应能看到以下关键行：

  [Change of Pace - Costume v1.7.1] loaded.
  [Init] Unity 2022.3.x | BepInEx 5.4.x
  [Init] Harmony patches applied: 9 method(s).
  [Patch] DecorationListUI.Setup triggered.
  [Patch] _scrollRect OK: 'DecorationScrollView' (content: 'Content')
  [Inject] Enter: scrollRect='...' content='...'
  [Inject] Complete: skins=5, options=3, hair=4, decor=3, ...
  [Patch] DecorationListUI.Activate triggered.

根据日志可以快速定位问题：

  · 没有 "[Init] Harmony patches applied" —— 补丁挂载失败，多半是游戏更新导致，请把日志发给作者。
  · 有补丁日志但看不到 "[Patch] DecorationListUI.Setup triggered" —— 装饰面板的初始化没有被触发。
  · 出现 "[Inject] FAILED" 或 "[Patch] '_scrollRect' not found" —— 请把 ERROR 行及其前后的几行日志发给作者。
  · 能看到 "Complete" 但面板里依然没有 Mod 区域 —— 请一并描述游戏内看到的现象。

[Logs & Troubleshooting]

If the mod features do not appear in-game, follow these steps to get the log:

1. Log file location: <game folder>\BepInEx\LogOutput.log.
2. Close the game normally (do not force-kill it), then open the file with Notepad; you may also send the whole file to the author.
3. Search the log for "Change of Pace - Costume" and send the block of lines containing that keyword.

A healthy log contains these key lines:

  [Change of Pace - Costume v1.7.1] loaded.
  [Init] Unity 2022.3.x | BepInEx 5.4.x
  [Init] Harmony patches applied: 9 method(s).
  [Patch] DecorationListUI.Setup triggered.
  [Patch] _scrollRect OK: 'DecorationScrollView' (content: 'Content')
  [Inject] Enter: scrollRect='...' content='...'
  [Inject] Complete: skins=5, options=3, hair=4, decor=3, ...
  [Patch] DecorationListUI.Activate triggered.

Quick triage from the log:

  · No "[Init] Harmony patches applied" — patch binding failed, most likely due to a game update; send the log to the author.
  · Patches present but no "[Patch] DecorationListUI.Setup triggered" — the decoration panel setup was never invoked.
  · "[Inject] FAILED" or "[Patch] '_scrollRect' not found" — send the ERROR line(s) and surrounding lines to the author.
  · "Complete" appears but the mod area is still missing in the panel — describe what you see in-game as well.

【常见问题】

问：升级 Mod 版本后，之前的设置会丢失吗？答：不会。Mod 设置保存在注册表（HKCU\Software\Nestopi\Chill With You），与游戏主存档完全独立。发型选择写在游戏自己的当日存档里，也会保留。
问：发型会一直保持吗？答：遵循游戏默认规则，一天内保持、每日轮换。服装的"一生"不受影响。
问：戴着贝雷帽为什么不能改发型？答：贝雷帽是特殊发型，戴着时无法修改发型。摘帽后可以再换。圣诞帽不限制发型。
问：戴上帽子后猫耳耳机消失了？答：这是正常设计——头部装饰会临时替换猫耳耳机，摘帽后不会自动戴回。
问：圣诞帽和贝雷帽可以同时戴吗？答：不可以。两者属于同一部位、互相排斥，开启其中一个会自动关闭另一个。

[FAQ]

Q: Will my settings survive a mod update? A: Yes. Mod settings are stored in the registry (HKCU\Software\Nestopi\Chill With You), fully independent of the game save. Hairstyle choice is stored in the game's own daily hair save and is kept as well.
Q: Does the hairstyle last forever? A: It follows the game's default rules: kept for the day, rotated daily. Costume "Forever" is unchanged.
Q: Why can't I change hair while wearing the beret? A: The beret is a special hair state — hair cannot be changed while it is worn. You can change hair again after removing it. The Christmas hat does not restrict hairstyle.
Q: Why do my cat-ear headphones disappear when wearing a hat? A: By design — headwear temporarily replaces them, and they do not return automatically after removal.
Q: Can I wear the Christmas hat and the beret at the same time? A: No. They share the same slot and are mutually exclusive — enabling one automatically disables the other.
